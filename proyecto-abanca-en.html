Abanca — illustration and acquisition campaigns

Xacobeo 21:22 illustrated series · Brand Days campaign for social media · display banners · art direction

Client — Abanca · Banking

My role — Social media and banner design, illustration and art direction

The Brand Days campaign brings payroll and travel offers to social media and display banners with direct language: a short message, real photography and a clear call to action. Each piece adapts to square, story and desktop banner formats without losing legibility.

The display banners keep the same visual language —warm photography and bold typography— so the brand reads the same in a feed as on the homepage of a digital newspaper.

Each campaign started from an idea from the creative team. I'd first design the main banner, which set the tone and message; once approved, I'd select the images that reinforced it best and develop the rest of the adaptations. Both the Brand Days pieces and the illustrations were approved and published on the bank's social channels.
