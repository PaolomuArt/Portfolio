I'm a product and brand designer based between Madrid and wherever the project needs me. I started out doing identity work for small studios, then drifted into product design once I realized brand and interface are really the same problem at different zoom levels. These days I split my time between early-stage startups and the occasional brand refresh for people who've outgrown their first logo.
