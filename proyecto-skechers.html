Skechers — system and format adaptation

Technical template system · Millennium Estoril Open sponsorship · Instagram stories · motion graphics · AI product imagery

The brief — Skechers sends a short brief: which shoes to sell, for which promo or season, and the mood they want. Sometimes they include the copy. Everything else is on us.

My role — AI imagery, template design, story design, motion and video editing. Around 6 pieces a week for Skechers, video and static.

How it works — Brief → first proposal → feedback rounds → final assets.

Result — I don't see the campaign numbers, since the media team runs the ads. But Skechers keeps coming back with new briefs every week, and the template system lets new shoe technologies launch quickly without starting from zero.

Client — Skechers · Sports retail

Cover — AI-generated key visual

Objective — Build a recognizable technical template system for Skechers while also producing creative pieces able to stand out on social media

My role — Template systems, motion graphics and AI product imagery

Result — A reusable technical template system across several models and technologies, plus an AI-generated product photography line for the Millennium Estoril Open sponsorship

Skechers needed two things at once: recurring pieces produced at scale and with consistency, and creative pieces able to stand out on social media. For the first, I designed a technical template system that presents each technology the same way, so the audience can recognize and compare it at a glance.

For the second, motion graphics and, later, AI. For much of the project, pieces were built using the available product photography, and that worked well. AI opened up a different way of working: designing my own photo shoots, with the shoes in new poses and settings. Both stages live side by side on this page.
