Skechers — system and format adaptation

Technical template system · Millennium Estoril Open sponsorship · Instagram stories · motion graphics · AI product imagery

Client Skechers · Sports retail

My role Template systems, motion graphics, AI product imagery and video editing

Objective Produce recurring pieces at scale and with consistency, plus creative pieces able to stand out on social media

Technical template system Presents each shoe technology the same way, so people can recognize and compare them at a glance. Started with GoRun, now used across other technologies.

Motion graphics Animated stories and the reel of the creative line.

AI product imagery My own photo shoots with the shoes in new poses and settings, generated in Runway from real shoe silhouettes and finished in Photoshop.

Also Promo stories built from product photography, and quick stories for key dates like Black Friday and Blue Monday.

How it worked Short brief (shoes, season, mood, sometimes the copy) → first proposal → feedback rounds → final assets. Around 6 pieces a week.

Result A reusable template system across several models and technologies, and an AI imagery line used for the Millennium Estoril Open sponsorship, among others. Skechers keeps sending new briefs every week.

Millennium Estoril Open — visual direction for the sponsorship's digital pieces and design of the stories and product content series.

Technical template — three examples

One template adapted to three different technologies: Glide-Step Flex, Hyperburst Cushioning and M-Strike · design and layout for each version.

Reusable technical template

Reusable technical template system, adapted to five shoe models · design and layout for each version.

Stories — Instagram examples

Instagram story examples: Estoril Open giveaway, newsletter campaign and Blue Monday · design for each piece.
