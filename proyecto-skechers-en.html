Skechers — system and format adaptation

Technical template system · Millennium Estoril Open sponsorship · Instagram stories · motion graphics · AI product imagery

Client — Skechers · Sports retail

My role — Template systems, motion graphics and AI product imagery

Skechers needed two things at once: recurring pieces produced at scale and with consistency, and creative pieces able to stand out on social media. For the first, I designed a technical template system that presents each technology the same way, so the audience can recognize and compare it at a glance.

For the second, motion graphics and, later, AI. For much of the project, pieces were built using the available product photography, and that worked well. AI opened up a different way of working: designing my own photo shoots, with the shoes in new poses and settings. Both stages live side by side on this page.

Millennium Estoril Open — visual direction for the sponsorship's digital pieces and design of the stories and product content series.
