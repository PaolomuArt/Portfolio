Alhambra — Instagram, festivals and music

The brief — We run Alhambra's Instagram. The brand works with music artists and festivals, and both care a lot about how things look. Alhambra prefers a human feel, so we use very little AI: mostly photo and video, heavily edited in Photoshop and the Adobe suite.

My role — Social media content, posters and video editing.

What I did —

  • Festival collaborations. Posts and stories for Granada Sound, Hondo and other festivals, mixing each festival's look with Alhambra's style.
  • Giveaways and artist content. With musicians and creators, plus the hand-painted tableware project with Asís Perales and Fundación Fajalauza.
  • Video. Most of what we make is video; I edit and design the pieces (see reel).

How it worked — Granada Sound post: one version in the festival's colours, one more corporate, and the final "Vive Granada Sound" in between. Options, feedback, refinement.

Result — Alhambra keeps the collaborations coming, with new creators, artists and festivals every season.
