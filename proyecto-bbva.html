BBVA: Email, animated banners, app and social across Spain, Italy and Germany

The brief — BBVA sends the copy and the message, for example "we want a visual that shows this offer." Everything else we build ourselves, following BBVA's visual language and brand rules.

What I did —

  • BBVA Spain (Digital Sales). Several years designing and building emails, plus PDFs and banners for digital sales campaigns.
  • BBVA Italy. Worked on the 500K-customer campaign. Designed and animated banners in Adobe Animate, adapted to every format and size limit, and prepared them for Amazon Ads with click tracking. Also HTML5 pieces, emails and the move to the new rebranding.
  • BBVA Germany. Banners for the launch campaign.
  • App Store screens. Designed the app campaign screens for Italy and Germany in every format, both before and after the rebranding.
  • Social media (BBVA Italy). Once we took over all of BBVA Italy's channels, I designed and animated posts, stories, Reels and TikTok formats.
  • AI imagery. Created campaign images with AI (prompts with Claude and others, generation in Midjourney), such as the hand holding the card, then finished them to match BBVA's brand.
  • Rebranding. Redesigned banners, emails, App Store screens and social templates to the new identity while campaigns kept running.

Range — HTML and CSS emails, animated HTML5 banners, static banners, AI images, edited PDFs, App Store screens, and social content for Instagram, Reels and TikTok.

Result — The Italy campaign helped reach 500K customers, and BBVA Germany went on to win banking awards there after the launch campaign. Our work kept expanding: from emails in Spain, to campaigns in Italy and Germany, to running all of BBVA Italy's social channels.

Client — BBVA · Spain, Italy and Germany

I worked with BBVA across three distinct moments: reaching 500,000 customers in Italy, working with the digital sales team in Spain, and the brand's launch in Germany.

Many pieces started from briefs that were just a few lines long. From there, I'd define the composition, design the lead piece, animate it in Adobe Animate and adapt it to each campaign's different formats.

Two rounds of App Store screens, two different strategies. The first presents the app's features one by one —biometrics, cashback or dynamic CVV— with real mockup photography. The second, after the rebrand, builds a brand narrative: 100% Extraordinary, 100% Secure, 100% Savings, 100% Freedom and 100% BBVA.

That same visual approach carried over to the launch campaigns in Italy and Germany and to paid social content: one identity, adapted to different markets and formats.
