BBVA — from explaining features to selling a brand idea

App Store screens (Spain) · launch campaigns (Italy and Germany) · paid social content · motion showreel

Client BBVA · Spain, Italy and Germany

My role Design and motion for digital campaigns: emails, animated banners, App Store screens, social media and AI imagery

Objective Hold one consistent brand identity across digital sales in Spain, reaching 500,000 customers in Italy, and the launch in Germany

Spain Several years designing and building emails for the digital sales team, plus PDFs and banners.

Animated banners Designed the lead piece, animated it in Adobe Animate, adapted it to every format and set it up in Amazon Ads with click tracking.

App Store screens Two rounds, two strategies. First, features one by one (biometrics, cashback, dynamic CVV). After the rebrand, a brand narrative: 100% Extraordinary, Secure, Savings, Freedom and BBVA.

Italy and Germany Launch campaigns and paid social. For BBVA Italy we later took over all social channels: posts, stories, Reels and TikTok, including AI images.

How it worked Brief of a few lines with the copy → I define the composition → design the lead piece → animate → adapt to every format.

Result The Italy campaign helped reach 500,000 customers, and BBVA Germany went on to win banking awards after the launch. Two App Store systems and one brand language across three markets.
