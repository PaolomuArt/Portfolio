Client — Repsol · Energy

My role — Design lead for internal communications and LinkedIn

As design lead, I was in charge of the intranet design and employee news pages, along with visual content for LinkedIn: infographics, illustrations and videos.

Much of this work belongs to internal communications and can't be shown. Here I've gathered the pieces I can share.

One infographic, two brand systems. The piece about the synergies between generative AI and data scientists was redesigned after Repsol's rebrand: the content stays the same while the visual language changes, from a corporate teal palette to a warmer system with its own illustrated mascots.

That same ability to keep a coherent system applies to internal comms in motion: an internal mobility video that animates seven job openings as cards, and LinkedIn content that translates technical topics —like the evolution of SEO in the AI era— into a readable format without losing rigor.
