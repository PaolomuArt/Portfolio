Alhambra — social media and video editing

Alhambra Especial x Asís Perales x Fundación Fajalauza · festival sponsorships · video editing for Instagram and TikTok

Client — Alhambra · Beverages

My role — Social media content, posters and video editing

My work with Alhambra spanned very different projects: special collaborations, like the hand-painted tableware alongside Asís Perales and Fundación Fajalauza; festival sponsorships, like Hondo Festival and Granada Sound; and day-to-day social media coverage. Each campaign had its own visual language, always within the brand's identity.

Much of that content lived in video: event recaps, interviews and pieces for stories that brought movement to photographs and ideas that started out static.
